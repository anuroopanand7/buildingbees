# India: personal data and GST, in plain words

- Say what data you take and why, before you take it. Ask: which details, and what for?
- Consent is its own clear step, not buried in terms. Ask: where do people say yes, in what words?
- People can withdraw consent as easily as they gave it. Ask: where is the stop button, and what happens then?
- Under-18s need a parent or guardian to agree. Ask: will under-18s use this, and how is an adult's yes checked?
- Keep data only while needed, then delete it. Ask: how long for each kind of data?
- People need someone to complain to. Ask: who answers, and which email or phone do we show?
- If data leaks, people must be told. Ask: what will you say, and how will you reach them?
- A GST invoice carries the seller's GSTIN and an HSN code (goods) or SAC code (services). Ask: goods or services, and is there a GSTIN?
- Same state: tax splits into CGST plus SGST. Different states: IGST. Ask: where is the seller, where is the buyer?
- Ask whether the seller is GST registered at all. Never state fines, deadlines or section numbers.
