# Requirements say what and why, never which

- Write what the person does and why it matters. Ask: what should they be able to do, and why?
- Never name a framework, database, cloud, vendor or language in a requirement. Test: if we swapped the tool tomorrow, is the line still true?
- If the founder names a technology, record it as a founder preference. Ask: is that a must, or your preference?
- Ask what outcome sits behind the tool name. Ask: what would be different for the user?
- Keep the numbers that matter (time, count, money). Ask: how fast, how many, how much?
- Mark each line must, should or later. Ask: launch day, or after?
- Use the founder's own words for things. Ask: what do you call this?
